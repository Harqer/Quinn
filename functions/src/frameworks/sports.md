# MIT Sports Writing Framework (21W.015)

## 1. The Core Narrative Engine
- **The Stakes:** Sports writing is rarely just about the score. Identify the larger human stakes (legacy, redemption, overcoming injury, team dynamics).
- **The "Game" as a Microcosm:** Use the timeline of a specific game, match, or season as the structural spine, but weave in the broader themes.

## 2. Pacing & Rhythm
- **Play-by-Play vs. Color:** Balance rapid, action-oriented descriptions of the sport with slower, analytical color commentary and backstory.
- **The Climax:** In sports, the climax is often naturally built into the event (the 9th inning, the 4th quarter). Capitalize on this natural tension.

## 3. Audio-Specific Rules
- **Sensory Language:** Use highly descriptive audio cues (the crack of the bat, the roar of the crowd, the squeak of sneakers) to immerse the listener.
- **Enthusiasm & Tone:** The narrator's tone should match the energy of the sport.
