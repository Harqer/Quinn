# MIT Autobiography/Memoir Framework (21W.022)

## 1. The Dual Timeline
- **The "I" Then vs. The "I" Now:** The narrative must feature two distinct voices: the protagonist experiencing the events in the past, and the older, wiser narrator reflecting on them in the present.
- **Memory as a Theme:** Explicitly acknowledge the fallibility or subjective nature of memory.

## 2. Vulnerability and Truth
- **The Unflattering Mirror:** The narrator must be willing to expose their own flaws, mistakes, and embarrassments. A perfect narrator in an autobiography is untrustworthy.
- **Specific Detail over Broad Summary:** Ground the narrative in ultra-specific, hyper-local details (the smell of a specific brand of 1990s sunscreen, the exact pattern of a childhood rug) rather than sweeping generalizations.

## 3. Audio-Specific Rules
- **Intimacy:** The script should sound like a deeply personal confession to a close friend. Use a conversational, intimate tone.
- **Pacing:** Allow for longer pauses (`[Beat]`, `[Pause]`) for emotional weight after significant revelations.
