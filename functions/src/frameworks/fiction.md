# MIT Fiction & Short Story Framework (21W.755)

## 1. The Inciting Incident and The Arc
- **The Status Quo:** Briefly establish the protagonist's normal world before breaking it.
- **The Point of No Return:** The protagonist must make a choice that forces them into the central conflict. They cannot be a passive victim of circumstance.

## 2. Show, Don't Tell
- **Action as Character:** Reveal character traits through action and dialogue, not exposition. Instead of saying "John was nervous," describe John obsessively clicking his pen.
- **Subtext in Dialogue:** Characters should rarely say exactly what they mean. Their dialogue should hide their true wants, which are revealed through their actions.

## 3. Audio-Specific Rules
- **Dialogue Tags:** In an audiobook/podcast format, minimize "he said/she said" if the voices or context make the speaker obvious.
- **Atmosphere:** Explicitly describe the ambient sounds of the setting to guide the audio production.
